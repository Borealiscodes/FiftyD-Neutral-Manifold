Traversal rules define how you move inside the neutral 50‑dimensional manifold. They don’t tell you why you’re walking — only how to walk in a strictly geometric, system‑agnostic way.

---

🚶‍♂️ What Traversal Is
Traversal is:

- updating a coordinate  
- by a small, bounded step  
- in a chosen direction  
- with reversible math  
- inside a neutral space  

It’s the geometry equivalent of “take one step forward.”

---

🔁 Reversibility
Every traversal step is designed to be:

- reversible  
- predictable  
- bounded  
- non‑semantic  

This ensures the manifold stays neutral and stable.

---

🧭 How Traversal Works
The traversal engine:

- takes a Coordinate50D  
- applies a step vector  
- clamps values to a safe numeric range  
- returns a new coordinate  

No semantics.  
No interpretation.  
No “meaning” behind the movement.

Just math.
